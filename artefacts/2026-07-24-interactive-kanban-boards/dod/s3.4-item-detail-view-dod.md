## DoD Observations

1. The route/identifier investigation (AC1's Architecture Constraint) surfaced a genuine, separate access-control finding: `/features/:slug` (the alternative candidate destination) has NO tenant-ownership check at all today — confirming `/journey/:id` (which already had one, just unwired) was the correct and safer choice. **Tag as `/improve` candidate:** `/features/:slug`'s missing tenant check is a real, standing gap outside this story's own scope (it doesn't gate access via THIS story's new entry point, but it remains reachable directly) — worth a follow-up story to add the same guard there.
2. This story's implementation was salvaged from a dispatched agent that died mid-investigation twice and had its worktree cross-contaminated with S3.3's own changes (documented in this session's capture-log.md) — a good example of extracting real, valuable partial work (the route investigation itself) rather than discarding it and starting over, once the contamination was correctly diagnosed and separated.
