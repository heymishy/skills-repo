# Decisions: staging flake fixes (sfx-s1)

## D1. Fix the cold-start flake at its cause with two budgets; instrument the other flake instead of guessing a fix (2026-11-21)

**Context:** the two ledger entries (#1181) need a decision by 2026-12-05. `a1` AC1's single 5-second check is the first request a freshly deployed app receives, so it includes the cold start. `bri-s3.3` AC1 calls a page that renders every tenant with one plan lookup per tenant on a shared 512 MB staging machine, but nothing records how long the call takes or how many tenants it renders, so the cause is a hypothesis.
**Decision (operator, 2026-11-21, "Yes" to the proposal):** (a) `a1` AC1 judges its first call against a 15 s cold-start budget and a second, warm call against the unchanged 5 s requirement, through a small tested helper; (b) `bri-s3.3` AC1 prints the status, elapsed time and tenant row count of each `/admin/credits` call (and rethrows any error), changing no assertion; (c) the ledger note records the 2026-11-21 occurrence. Batching the per-tenant lookups is a separate story, only if the data supports it.
**Rationale:** (a) removes the cause without weakening the requirement it protects (steady-state latency) and still fails a broken deploy; (b) turns each future occurrence into evidence instead of a guess, and costs nothing when the test passes; guessing a product change for a flake whose cause is unmeasured would trade one unknown for another.
**Alternatives rejected:** raise the single budget to 15 s (loses the steady-state requirement); a quiet retry loop inside `bri-s3.3` AC1 (the global `--retries=1` already covers a one-off and the quiet retry would hide the evidence); batch the `/admin/credits` queries now (product change without evidence of cause, in an area with other tests); delete or renew the ledger entries now (the operator's review, by 2026-12-05).
**Risk accepted:** the cold-start budget could be too generous or too tight; it is a ceiling chosen above any observed cold call and is revisited with data. The diagnostics add a few console lines per run.
**Operator go-ahead (2026-11-21):** "Yes" to the `a1` cold/warm fix and the `bri-s3.3` instrumentation proposed on 2026-11-21.
