## Goal

Operators can switch to a Customer experience view to see emotion, pain points, and opportunities as annotation rows, and can see per-stage health indicators (✅ / ⚠️ / ❌) computed from feature and metric coverage, plus a summary bar showing overall journey metric coverage. This epic surfaces the journey health signal that makes the canvas actionable for tech leads and squad leads.
